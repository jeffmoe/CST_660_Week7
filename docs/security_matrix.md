**Stated regulatory scope: the `base` scenario of the assumptions register.**

A-REG-* assumptions per scenario. Change an assumption, re-run the script, and this
document changes with it. Legal references describe obligations; interpreting them
for Aurora is a Legal decision made at the gates below.

### Windows that change with regulatory scope

- **R-01** (Subscriber identity, contact and subscription history): low = 36 months (3 yr), base = 36 months (3 yr), high = 24 months (2 yr). Win-back and support for returning customers (3 years); capped at 24 months under GDPR storage limitation; deletion request overrides.
- **R-06** (Raw identified clickstream (device/cookie IDs, IP, user agent)): low = 13 months, base = 13 months, high = 25 months. Hot raw retention from the assumptions register; GDPR-scope cap follows CNIL audience-measurement guidance (25 months).
- **R-08** (Email and SMS engagement events): low = 36 months (3 yr), base = 36 months (3 yr), high = 24 months (2 yr). Engagement features for churn model and deliverability (3 years of seasonality); capped at 24 months under GDPR.
- **R-10** (Raw support transcripts and tickets (helpdesk system of record)): low = 36 months (3 yr), base = 36 months (3 yr), high = 24 months (2 yr). Support history and RAG corpus source (3 years); capped at 24 months under GDPR; never copied unredacted into the warehouse.
- **R-11** (Redacted transcripts and retrieval chunks (warehouse)): low = 36 months (3 yr), base = 36 months (3 yr), high = 24 months (2 yr). A derived copy may not outlive its source.
- **R-12** (Transcript embeddings / vector index): low = 36 months (3 yr), base = 36 months (3 yr), high = 24 months (2 yr). Embeddings can be inverted to recover text (E-SEC-04), so they follow the text they encode.
- **R-14** (Wellness-quiz answers and health-interest tags): low = 24 months (2 yr), base = 3 months, high = 3 months. Personalization for returning customers wants 24 months; when the data is consumer health data (A-REG-02) it is minimized to 3 months; deletion request overrides.
- **R-15** (Churn features and risk scores (pseudonymous)): low = 37 months, base = 37 months, high = 25 months. Three years of history plus one month for the churn model's training window; capped at 25 months under GDPR.

**Coupling check:** raw clickstream retention (R-06) is never shorter than the A-STO-02
hot-retention window the storage projections assume, in any scenario.
