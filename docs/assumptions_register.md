## Basis and validation

- **A-GRW-01 Starting active subscribers** — Current billing-system count of active subscriptions (leadership brief). *Validation:* Reconcile against billing system on FY start date.
- **A-GRW-02 End-of-year active subscribers** — Base is the leadership target. Low assumes 70% attainment; high assumes a ~30% overshoot from a successful retail/marketplace launch. Sizing is done against HIGH so the platform is not re-architected mid-year. *Validation:* Monthly variance check of actuals vs curve; re-baseline if off by >15% for 2 consecutive months.
- **A-GRW-03 Growth curve shape** — Constant month-over-month compound growth between start and end values (base = 16.5%/mo). Front-loaded S-curves would bring peak volumes forward; compound is the conservative choice for budget timing. *Validation:* Fit compound vs logistic to the first 3 months of actuals.
- **A-GRW-04 Monthly gross churn rate** — Typical range for DTC consumable subscriptions; drives the number of cancelled-subscriber histories retained for ML training. Must be replaced by Aurora's measured cohort churn. *Validation:* Compute trailing 12-month cohort churn from billing data.
- **A-EVT-01 Daily active share of subscribers** — Supplements are low-touch; most subscribers visit only around renewal, shipping and promotions. Estimate pending product analytics export. *Validation:* 30-day DAU/subscriber ratio from web/app analytics.
- **A-EVT-02 Behavioral events per daily-active subscriber** — Page views, clicks, cart and account actions per session from current tag plan. *Validation:* Count events per session in a 14-day sample of the clickstream.
- **A-EVT-03 Lifecycle + messaging events per subscriber** — Billing/renewal/skip/pause events (~0.1/day) plus email & SMS sends/opens/clicks (~1.5/day). Applies to all subscribers, not only DAU. *Validation:* ESP export + billing webhook counts for one month.
- **A-EVT-04 Peak-to-average daily multiplier** — Promotional days (Black Friday, New Year) vs. ordinary days. *Validation:* Compare last BFCM daily event count to trailing 30-day mean.
- **A-EVT-05 Average raw event payload size** — JSON events with context block (device, UTM, consent flags). *Validation:* Measure mean payload over a 1-day sample.
- **A-TRN-01 Monthly contact rate** — Share of subscribers contacting support per month (chat, email, voice-to-text); shipping, billing and cancellation are the dominant drivers. *Validation:* Helpdesk ticket count / active subscribers for trailing 6 months.
- **A-TRN-02 Average transcript length** — Chat/email threads after signature and boilerplate stripping; voice transcripts are longer. *Validation:* Tokenize a 1,000-transcript sample with the chosen embedding tokenizer.
- **A-TRN-03 Historical transcript backfill** — ~18 months of helpdesk history at the pre-growth subscriber base. *Validation:* Helpdesk export row count.
- **A-TRN-04 Retrieval chunk size** — Common RAG default; final value set by retrieval-quality testing. *Validation:* Recall@k evaluation on labeled Aurora queries (see C-AI-01).
- **A-TRN-05 Embedding dimensionality** — Range of common commercial and open embedding models. *Validation:* Fixed once embedding model is selected.
- **A-STO-01 Columnar compression ratio (JSON -> Parquet/warehouse)** — Typical for semi-structured clickstream in columnar formats. HIGH scenario uses the LOW ratio (worst case) when sizing. *Validation:* Load 1 day of events and compare raw vs stored bytes.
- **A-STO-02 Raw event hot retention** — 13 months supports year-over-year comparison; older raw data moves to archive tier. *Validation:* Policy sign-off with Legal (privacy retention limits).
- **A-STO-03 Modeled-layer overhead vs compressed raw** — Staging, intermediate, marts, snapshots and feature tables as a multiple of raw. *Validation:* Measure after first 3 months of dbt models in production.
- **A-STO-04 Vector index overhead vs raw vectors** — HNSW graph + metadata overhead on top of float32 vectors. *Validation:* Inspect index size after backfill load.
- **A-OPS-01 Data team size (analytics + data + ML engineers)** — Headcount stays roughly flat while subscribers grow ~6x; no dedicated platform/SRE engineer. Drives the weight given to operational burden. *Validation:* Confirm approved headcount plan with Finance.
- **A-OPS-02 Daily freshness SLA for retention marts and churn features** — Retention team's morning stand-up works the at-risk list; the churn model scores nightly. No current consumer has an intraday requirement on record. *Validation:* Written SLA agreed with retention and ML leads.
- **A-OPS-03 Late-arriving event window** — Mobile offline events, payment-processor retries and webhook redelivery arrive after the event date; incremental models must reprocess this window. *Validation:* Distribution of (ingested_at - event_time) over 30 days of events.
- **A-GOV-01 Consent scope for subscriber data** — Current consent language covers order fulfillment and service communications and says nothing about third-party processing. Sending subscriber data or transcripts to an external model provider is therefore unconfirmed until Legal reviews it. *Validation:* Legal opinion on whether a processor DPA covers LLM inference, or whether consent must change.
- **A-BUD-01 Annual platform run-rate ceiling (excl. headcount)** — Placeholder pending Finance confirmation. Base sized at ~0.15% of projected year-end ARR (250k subs x ~$40/mo x 12). Hard ceiling = base; high is the escalation threshold requiring CFO approval. *Validation:* Written confirmation from Finance before ADR approval.
- **A-BUD-02 Budget allocation by component** — Starting split for guardrails; rebalanced quarterly against actual billing. *Validation:* Monthly cloud bill tagged by component.
- **A-BUD-03 Cost per retention-assistant conversation** — Retrieval + generation with prompt caching. Depends on model choice, turns per conversation and context size; must be measured. *Validation:* Instrumented pilot with token logging (see C-AI-04).
